About

Summary & Research focus

My work focuses on making additive manufacturing more reliable, measurable, and qualification-ready. I work across metal and polymer processes—including L-PBF, WAAM, and FDM—and combine process development, materials characterization, nondestructive testing, and data-driven analysis. Through experimental research and aerospace-adjacent qualification studies, I connect manufacturing processes to defects, material properties, and ultimately part performance.

Research projects & contributions

Work spanning the additive manufacturing pipeline
Metal AM · L-PBF

Investigates the influence of process parameters, build plate variability & interruptions on micro-mechanical behavior and property variation in SS316L and Ti6Al4V parts, supporting Federal Aviation Authority (FAA)-aligned qualification and inspection goals. Uses Magics, Autodesk Netfabb, EOS Print and Altair Inspire for support generation and build preparation.

Polymer AM · ULTEM 9085

Aerospace qualification style specimen fabrication via Stratasys FDM, with dimensional inspection, mechanical testing, and NDE to study print/part quality and defect formation.

Mechanical testing

Tensile, compression, and bending testing; nanoindentation-based hardness/modulus mapping; SEM microstructural analysis; profilometry; wire-EDM sample prep.

ML property classification

Data-driven workflows integrating experimental measurements, statistical analysis, and visualization to predict and classify property changes in AM parts.

NDT & in-situ monitoring

Ultrasound, Ultrasound Thermography, Acoustic Emission, connecting nondestructive signals to stiffness and defect-related behavior in support of quality control.
